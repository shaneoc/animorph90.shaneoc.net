The Original Site

Evidence

I have found some information related to the site by searching in the Internet Archive Wayback Machine. I found four files that I wrote myself. I have now added those files to the archive section of my main site. The files I found were:

  • The last version of the page at animorph90.hypermart.net, which only contained a redirect link.
  • A file for the Animorph90's Animorphs Active Channel, which was an Internet Explorer feature that I was experimenting with at the time. It looks like I used it to create a screensaver.
  • A file for the Animorph90's Animorphs Desktop Component.
  • A file which was downloaded after I moved the site to the ani-morphs.com domain name and shows an example of my use of the <frameset> tag. You have to click the "view source" link to see the tag.

Aside from this, I also found pages that were written by others who were assisting with the site after it had been moved to ani-morphs.com. I have not copied those pages into the personal archive on my website. One of the pages includes a message from me at the top of the page, which reads:

NOTE: This is a message from the origional owner of this site (Animorph90 (You know, the guy who bought a domain, used it for a year, and then gave away without telling the visitors)). I just wanted to say that the people who have owned this site for the past few months forgot to mention that I also have a different site at http://www.dev-world.net/. It will be finished in about a week or two, I hope. (And no, it won't be filled with stuff about Harry Potter, Cars, and other crap like that even though the site has nothing to do with it.)

Oh, and by the way, this site will be closing on September 17, 2000

I also found one other page in the Internet Archive that is interesting. On June 11, 2011, this page was captured by the Internet Archive, for an unknown reason. The page itself is a generic domain parking page created by GoDaddy, however this is the address:

http://www.ani-morphs.com/midi/secret_agent_man.mid

The file being referenced, secret_agent_man.mid, appears to be one of the MIDI files that would play when someone would visit my website. The fact that the Internet Archive would try to access this address in 2011, many years after my site had been taken offline, seems like it might be a sign that my website lives on somewhere somehow. I wonder if it is possible to get it back.
